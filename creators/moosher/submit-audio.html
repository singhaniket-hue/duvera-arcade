Found a good reaction?

Send a Moosher moment.

A great “let’s go”, a laugh, a perfectly timed reaction. Share a short original clip for the arcade owner to review.

MP3 or uncompressed WAV · 0.2–15 seconds · up to 1 MiB. Preview your cut before sending. Your upload stays private and is never added to a game automatically.

What happens next?

The owner listens privately and may keep your clip for a later game update. Audio rights stay with their owners; submitting does not transfer ownership or make a clip open source.

Unreviewed clips expire after 30 days. Approved candidates expire after 90 days unless downloaded. Rejected clips are deleted. Daily upload limits apply; a daily salted network-address hash is retained until the next day to limit spam. Cloudflare may retain recovery copies for up to 30 days after deletion. Do not include personal or private recordings.

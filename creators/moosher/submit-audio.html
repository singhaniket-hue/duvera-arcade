Clip submissions

Send a clip for review.

Heard a Moosher moment that would fit the games? Upload your cut and link to the original video so the owner can check it.

MP3 or uncompressed WAV · 0.2–15 seconds · up to 1 MiB. Preview your cut before sending. Your upload stays private and is never added to a game automatically.

What happens next?

The owner can listen, reject a clip or approve it for download. Adding an approved clip to a game takes a separate update. Audio rights stay with their owners; submitting does not transfer ownership or make a clip open source.

Unreviewed clips are deleted after 30 days. Approved clips are deleted 90 days after approval, even if downloaded. Rejected clips are deleted when the owner rejects them.

Daily upload limits reduce spam. We keep a daily changing hash of your network address until the next day to enforce those limits. Cloudflare may keep recovery copies for up to 30 days after deletion. Please don’t send private recordings or personal information.

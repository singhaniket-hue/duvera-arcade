Game credits.

These games were created by independent developers. Duvera Arcade brings them together in one place; their original authors retain credit for their work.

Clumsy Bird

By Ellison Leão and contributors, built with melonJS. The original game files are unchanged.

Original repository · GPL-3.0 license · Original credits

2048

By Gabriele Cirulli and contributors. Based on 1024 by Veewo Studio and conceptually similar to Threes by Asher Vollmer. Includes the original Clear Sans font assets.

Original repository · MIT license

Hextris

By Logan Engstrom, Garrett Finucane, Noah Moroze, Michael Yang and contributors. Includes the original font and vendor assets. Advertising, analytics and external score reporting have been removed for this edition.

Original repository · GPL-3.0 license

Moosher creator edition

The optional Moosher Arcade is fan-made. Artwork generated with Higgsfield is based on the public @Moosherr channel image. Short audio candidates come from her edited stream highlights and still need human listening review. Creator media are not relicensed under the original game licenses. Sources and review status.
